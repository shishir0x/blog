Me sitting with my mother feels like nothing more than a 10-minute podcast about how I have failed to live up to their expectations and how I have become a disappointment in the eyes of society.

Every conversation somehow turns into a reminder of how I am not enough, how my choices have affected their image, and how much pressure they carry because of me. Instead of feeling like I am sitting with my family, it feels like I am sitting in front of a judge who is constantly reminding me of my mistakes.

It hurts because I don't want to be seen as a burden or a source of disappointment. I don't want my existence to feel like something that needs to be explained or defended. I want to feel like I am more than my achievements, my failures, or what people around us think about me.

Those conversations slowly make me feel like I don't belong here anymore. They make me want to leave this house as soon as I possibly can—not because I don't love my family, but because I feel exhausted from constantly trying to prove that I am worth something.

I want a place where I can breathe. A place where I can make mistakes, learn, and grow without feeling like every failure is a reflection of my family's reputation. A place where I can discover myself without constantly carrying the weight of everyone's expectations.

---

## 💔 Losing Hope in Love

Somewhere along the way, I also lost hope in relationships and love. I started believing that maybe love is something meant for other people, not me. Every disappointment, every rejection, and every moment where I felt like I wasn't someone's choice slowly changed the way I see myself.

I began questioning whether I am truly capable of being loved or whether I will always be the person who cares more, tries harder, and gets hurt in the end. The idea of finding someone who understands me, accepts me, and chooses me feels distant now. Not because I don't want love, but because I have started doubting whether I am someone worth loving.

---

## 🛑 Feeling Stuck

Looking back, it feels like I haven't achieved anything meaningful yet. I see people moving forward, building their careers, finding their purpose, and creating lives they are proud of, while I feel stuck in the same place, trying to understand who I am and where I belong.

The dreams I once had feel distant. The confidence I once carried has slowly been replaced by doubt, fear, and constant overthinking. I am tired of:
- 📉 Feeling like I am falling behind.
- 🔄 Comparing my journey with everyone else's.
- ❌ Feeling like every mistake defines who I am.

---

## 🛤️ Finding My Way Back

Maybe I haven't achieved everything I wanted yet. Maybe I am not where I thought I would be at this point in my life. But that does not mean my story is over.

I still have time to learn, to grow, and to become someone I can be proud of. Not someone who exists only to satisfy society's expectations, but someone who can look at himself and say:

> **"I tried. I survived. I kept going. And eventually, I became better."**

This journey might be slower than I expected, but it is still mine.
